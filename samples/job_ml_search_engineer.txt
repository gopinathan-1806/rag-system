Machine Learning Engineer - Search & Relevance
Northwind Labs

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are building the next generation of semantic search for enterprise knowledge. You will own retrieval quality end to end, from embeddings to ranking to evaluation.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design and ship retrieval and ranking pipelines using embeddings and vector databases.
- Build offline evaluation harnesses and run online A/B tests to improve relevance.
- Fine-tune and serve transformer models in production on Kubernetes.
- Partner with product to turn ambiguous problems into measurable ML objectives.

REQUIREMENTS
- 4+ years of professional software engineering experience, 2+ years in applied ML or search.
- Strong Python skills and experience with PyTorch.
- Hands-on experience with vector search (pgvector, FAISS, Elasticsearch or similar).
- Experience deploying services on Kubernetes.
- Experience designing evaluation metrics (NDCG, MRR) for ranking systems.

NICE TO HAVE
- Experience with LLM-based applications and RAG.
- Familiarity with MLflow or similar experiment tracking.
- Publications or open-source contributions in IR / NLP.
